About Black Velvet Stories 💜

Black Velvet Stories was created from a simple belief: Black indie authors deserve a beautiful space to tell their stories, be seen, and be respected.

For readers, it’s a cozy sanctuary for storytelling — built to feel premium, calm, and addictive in the best way. For authors, it’s a creator-first home with real tools, real community, and clear expectations.

Our mission

Honor the storyteller. Support the reader. Build community — with a premium reading experience.

Founder-led

Built by an indie author who understands the joy of creating and the frustration of being overlooked.

Where we’re going

Optional publishing services later — editing, covers, and support for authors who want to go bigger.

Whether you’re here to read, write, or explore — you belong here. 🖤

Content Guidelines 🖤

We celebrate creative freedom while protecting readers, authors, and the community. These guidelines apply to books, covers, titles, and comments.

Allowed (with labeling)

All genres welcome, including dark romance, paranormal, sci-fi, fantasy, thriller, and erotica/BDSM (Spicy Vault 😈) — with proper tags.

Required tags & warnings

Use genre tags and content warnings when relevant (violence, non-consent themes, self-harm references, etc.). Spicy Vault must be marked adult.

Not allowed

Child sexual content, sexual content involving minors, explicit incest, bestiality, instructions for violence/illegal harm, hate/harassment, or doxxing.

Creators: if you’re unsure how to tag a scene, tag it. Transparency builds trust.
